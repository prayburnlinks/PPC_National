Privacy Policy

PPC I Love My Church — official app of the Pentecostal Protestant Church (Pinkster Protestante Kerk)

Effective date: 14 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information the PPC I Love My Church app collects, why we collect it, and how it is protected. The app is operated by the Pentecostal Protestant Church (PPC National), and we handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

  • Account details — when you register we ask for your name, email address, phone number, congregation, and district, together with a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Church activity — event registrations, merchandise orders, and prayer requests you choose to submit through the app.
  • Proof of payment — if you register for a paid event or place an order, you may upload a photo or PDF of your payment confirmation.
  • Usage records — basic records such as when your account was created and last signed in.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or files beyond what you choose to upload, and the app contains no advertising or third-party marketing trackers.

A note on religious affiliation

Because this is a church app, your use of it can indicate your religious affiliation, which POPIA treats as special personal information. We only use this information to serve you as a member or visitor of the church community, and we never share it for any other purpose.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, including approval of leader and administrator roles.
  • To manage event registrations, merchandise orders, and their payment confirmation.
  • To share church news, documents, and notifications with you inside the app.
  • To respond to prayer requests submitted to the church.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Who can see your information

Access inside the church is limited by role: church administrators can view member records where needed to manage the app, and congregation leaders can view certain records (such as proof of payment) only for members of their own congregation. Other members cannot see your personal details.

Where your information is stored

The app is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage). Your information is stored on Google's servers, which may be located outside South Africa; Google provides contractual and technical safeguards for such processing, and access from our side is restricted by security rules. You can read more in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Records connected to payments (such as proof-of-payment uploads) may be retained for as long as the church's financial record-keeping requires. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal information except where we are required to keep it.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and database and file access is governed by server-side security rules that enforce the role-based access described above.

Children

The app is intended for members and visitors of the church community. Children under 18 should use the app with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To exercise any of these rights, or to have your account removed, contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
