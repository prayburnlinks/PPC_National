Delete Your Account

PPC I Love My Church — official app of the Pentecostal Protestant Church (Pinkster Protestante Kerk)

You can delete your PPC I Love My Church account and its data at any time, in either of the two ways below.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email us to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to prayburnl@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Send it from the email address you registered with, so we can confirm the account is yours. If you no longer use that address, include your full name, congregation and mobile number instead, and we will contact you to confirm.
  3. We will delete the account and reply to confirm once it is done.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

Deleted permanently: your sign-in credentials, your profile (name, email address, phone number, congregation, district and role), your prayer requests, your notifications, and any record of prayer you marked on other members' requests.

What is kept, and for how long

Event registrations and merchandise orders are kept, together with their amounts and proof-of-payment files, because the church is obliged to keep an accurate financial record of money received. Your identity is removed from them: your name is replaced with “Deleted member”, and because your profile and sign-in are destroyed, nothing left on those records identifies you. They are retained for as long as the church's financial record-keeping requires.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you rejoin later you will need to register again, and your past registrations and orders will not be restored to you.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
