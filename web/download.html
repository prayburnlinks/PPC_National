PPC I Love My Church

The official mobile app of the Pentecostal Protestant Church (Pinkster Protestante Kerk) — events, church documents, the church store, and more.

Android

Version 1.0.0 · Android 7.0 or newer

Download the app (APK)
  1. Tap Download the app above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask permission to install apps from this source — tap Settings and allow it.
  3. Tap Install, then open the app and sign in with your mobile number or email.

iPhone & iPad

Version 1.0.0 · iOS 15.1 or newer

Step 1 — Install Expo Go Step 2 — Open the church app
  1. Tap Step 1 and install Expo Go from the App Store (it is free).
  2. Come back to this page and tap Step 2. Choose Open when iPhone asks.
  3. The church app loads inside Expo Go. Sign in with your mobile number or email.

While we are testing, the iPhone version runs inside Expo Go, so you will see the Expo screen first each time. Open Expo Go and tap PPC I Love My Church under Recently opened to get back in. If Step 2 does not open, paste this into Expo Go → Enter URL manually: exp://u.expo.dev/4159b7fe-4875-4e33-a6e7-ab4b38b2b314?channel-name=expo-go&runtime-version=exposdk:54.0.0
